Questions people ask first.

Can you build this for my industry?
Yes — I work from your process, rather than a fixed industry template. We’ll look at your tools, customer journey and day-to-day work, then agree on something useful to automate. The industries in the demos are examples.
Is it only WhatsApp and Instagram?
I can build around website forms and chat, email, SMS, social messaging, CRMs, calendars and other apps with supported integrations. This includes inbound requests and outbound outreach or follow-ups. I confirm the channel support and messaging permissions before we settle the scope.
What if I need something more custom?
Bring me the problem, even if it isn’t listed here. I’m open to custom integrations, internal workflows, reporting and other automation projects. I’ll check the requirements, explain the options and agree on the work, timing and support with you.
Can my team take over?
Yes. A request for a person, an unknown answer or an exception can pause the assistant and assign someone a summary and next action. The workflow uses approved business information, and questions needing professional judgement go to the right person.
Who owns the data and connected accounts?
Your business keeps ownership of its accounts and records. I build around your CRM, sheet, calendar or agreed system, document the workflow and show your team how to manage it. Access, exports and handover are agreed before launch.
What does it cost?
Pricing on call. It depends on the workflow, channels, integrations, usage and ongoing support you need. I’ll separate my work from any software or messaging costs and confirm the scope before you commit.

What would you like to automate?

Tell me what takes too much time, where follow-ups get missed or which tools need to work together. We’ll explore a useful first project. No prep needed.
